University of Virginia

Hi! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science at the University of Virginia, advised by Dr. Thomas Hartvigsen. My research focuses on machine learning in ever-changing environments. Motivated by real-world needs in healthcare, I explore three research directions: (1) learning from dynamic data (e.g., time series), (2) continually updating pretrained models (e.g., model editing), and (3) evaluating models under varying data assumptions. My work has been published in ICML, KDD, Statistical Methods in Medical Research, Clinical Infectious Diseases, and other venues.
