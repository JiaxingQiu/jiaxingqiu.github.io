Jiaxing (Joy) Qiu

Ph.D. Candidate in Data Science
University of Virginia

Hi! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science at the University of Virginia, advised by Dr. Thomas Hartvigsen. My research focuses on machine learning in the ever-changing environments. Motivated by healthcare needs and insights, I explore three research directions: (1) learning from dynamic data, (2) continually updating pretrained models, and (3) adapting evaluations to varied assumptions. My work has been published in ICML, KDD, Statistical Methods in Medical Research, Clinical Infectious Diseases, and other venues.
